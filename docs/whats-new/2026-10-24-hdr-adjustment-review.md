### HDR edits: zoomed views and adjustments

- Zoomed in on an HDR edit, a part of the photo the HDR picture can't cover shows sharp instead of magnified and soft.
- Dragging a slider redraws only the photo you edit: the Before view and other photos in Compare stay as they are, and HDR drags do less work per step.
- A crop or rotation shows its new shape at once, and a preview that fails to render no longer leaves an outdated one on screen.

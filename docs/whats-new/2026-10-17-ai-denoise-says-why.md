### AI Denoise says why it's greyed out
- For a photo AI Denoise can't use, the Detail panel now says why under the greyed-out switch (and when you hover
  it) instead of saying nothing. A raw that opens as its embedded preview, such as a Nikon High Efficiency NEF,
  names the reason its sensor data isn't read; a JPEG or other non-raw file says AI Denoise works on raw files
  only.

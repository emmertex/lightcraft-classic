### Waveform and RGB Parade scopes
- The histogram's selector (or `view.histogram.mode`) has two more views. **Waveform** draws the photo's tones column
  by column, left to right as in the photo, with black at the bottom and white at the top, so you can see where in
  the frame a highlight or a shadow sits. **RGB Parade** draws red, green and blue the same way in three tracks side by
  side, which makes a colour cast in one part of the frame easy to spot. Both follow the Linear / Logarithmic scale.
